A sensitive thread can mix what a source has stated, what a recipient has concluded, and what still needs checking.

Before a recipient or staff member replies in Hush Line:

1. Read the full conversation.
2. List what the source has stated. Do not treat it as verified unless your team has checked it.
3. Mark the questions that remain open.
4. Decide which one fact or document would help with the next review.
5. Write a focused request, then remove anything you do not need the source to share.

This keeps the next reply tied to the conversation without asking for extra sensitive details.

Add this five-step check to the reply instructions for everyone who shares your Hush Line inbox.
