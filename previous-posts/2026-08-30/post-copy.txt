Email can announce a new tip; its contents can stay in Hush Line.

Before sharing a Hush Line tip line, answer these questions with recipients and staff:

1. Choose Hush Line inbox notifications for the people who will review messages there.
2. Decide which staff members also need email notifications.
3. Select whether email includes encrypted tip contents or only an alert.
4. Confirm delivery addresses and their PGP keys.

Use the least email content that still supports reliable review. Write down the decision before asking anyone to send sensitive details.
