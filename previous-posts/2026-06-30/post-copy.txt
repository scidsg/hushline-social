When a source submits a message, the next question is simple: what should they see while they wait?

In Hush Line, recipients and staff can open Settings, choose Message Statuses, and edit the Waiting status text. The visible note can say that the message was received, give a realistic reply window, and remind the sender that they can check the same page for an update.

Review this wording before the tip line is busy, then use Update Reply Text when the waiting message matches how your team actually handles intake.
