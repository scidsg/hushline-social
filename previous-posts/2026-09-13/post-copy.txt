LinkedIn (618/3000)
A staffing change should trigger a check of Notifications.

ISO 37002 calls for whistleblowing systems to be maintained and improved. In practice, recipients and staff can open Settings > Notifications whenever responsibilities change.

Confirm the current role for email notifications. Set the Hush Line inbox as the review location when that is enough. Send encrypted tip contents by email only when the role requires them. This keeps reports moving without mailing sensitive material to someone whose work has changed.

Make this check part of offboarding, and finish it while the handoff details are still current.
