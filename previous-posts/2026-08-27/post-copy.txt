A common intake mistake is leaving a reply-time promise in place after staffing or schedules change.

Hush Line’s Message Statuses settings let recipients and staff edit the Waiting status text shown on the temporary page a source can check after sending a message. Give a timeframe your team can meet, and tell the source to return to that page for an update.
