Sensitive disclosures can need protection for years. The encryption we use today has to account for tomorrow’s threats.

Hush Line v0.7.25 adds hybrid post-quantum OpenPGP recipient support, combining ML-KEM-768 with X25519 for supported recipient keys.

We verified three RFC 9980 key profiles across browser-side submission encryption and server-side encryption, with successful decryption between our JavaScript and Sequoia implementations. Our tests check that the hybrid algorithm was actually used, so a silent fallback to classical encryption cannot count as success.

This support applies to the tested OpenPGP recipient profiles. In-app chat remains classical, and existing classical recipient keys are not automatically upgraded.

Review the supported keys and compatibility limits before changing your encryption setup:
https://github.com/scidsg/hushline/blob/v0.7.25/docs/POST-QUANTUM-OPENPGP.md
