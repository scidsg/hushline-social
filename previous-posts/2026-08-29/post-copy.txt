LinkedIn (518/3000)
An encrypted export should be checked before it is needed.

For recipients and staff who share responsibility for a Hush Line tip line:

1. Choose who is responsible for account exports.
2. Open Advanced settings and select “Encrypt export with my PGP key.”
3. Use “Download My Data” to save the ZIP with CSV exports and encrypted PGP messages.
4. Check that the PGP key opens the export.
5. Store or delete the ZIP according to your team’s access and retention rules.

Put the next export check on your team calendar.
