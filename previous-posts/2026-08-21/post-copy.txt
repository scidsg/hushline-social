LinkedIn (620/3000)
Before a recipient account is closed, make the review a checklist:

1. Check what your team is allowed or required to retain.
2. If a copy is needed, select “Encrypt export with my PGP key,” then use “Download My Data.”
3. Store the ZIP only where authorized staff can access it.
4. Treat “Delete Account” as the final step. It cannot be undone.

For journalists, lawyers, educators, organizers, and other teams that receive sensitive tips, this turns an account handoff into a repeatable review instead of a last-minute decision.

Add these checks to your staff handoff process before anyone closes a Hush Line account.
