LinkedIn (523/3000)
Do not download Hush Line records to a shared computer just because you plan to move the file later.

In Advanced settings, Download My Data creates a ZIP with CSV exports and encrypted PGP messages. Recipients and staff can select “Encrypt export with my PGP key” before downloading. The team still needs to control where the ZIP is saved and who can access it.

Before downloading, choose the device and storage location, limit access to authorized staff, and set a date to review whether the file still needs to be kept.
