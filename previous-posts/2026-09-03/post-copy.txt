When one staff member hands an email review to another, the next person needs more than a yes or no.

A useful whistleblowing practice is to leave enough context for the next reviewer to understand what was checked. In Hush Line, recipients and staff can paste Raw Email Headers into Email Validation, read the Validation Summary, and Download Report.

That report can support a newsroom, law office, school, or community group during an internal handoff, without asking the next person to reconstruct the check from memory. It follows a plain ISO 37002 principle: handle reports properly and without avoidable delay.

Before the next email handoff, decide where authorized staff will store the downloaded report and who needs to read it.
