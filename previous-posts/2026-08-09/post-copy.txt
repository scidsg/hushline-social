LinkedIn (509/3000)
Sensitive disclosures are often read in stressful circumstances.

The Waiting field in Hush Line can hold ordinary words instead of language that only your organization recognizes.

A readability routine for recipients:
1. Lead with the action a person needs to take.
2. Use one idea per sentence.
3. Remove abbreviations and internal labels.
4. Ask a colleague outside your program to paraphrase the instructions.

If their version changes the meaning, revise the field until the instruction is unmistakable.
