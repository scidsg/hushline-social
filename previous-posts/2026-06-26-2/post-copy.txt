Before you contact someone through Hush Line, do a short source-safety check.

On the public Directory:
1. Use Tor Browser for greater anonymity.
2. Start with Verified or the category that fits your situation, such as Attorneys, Journalists, SecureDrop, or GlobaLeaks.
3. Search verified users by name or organization.
4. Open View Profile and read the details before you send anything sensitive.
5. Remember: an account is not required to send a message.
