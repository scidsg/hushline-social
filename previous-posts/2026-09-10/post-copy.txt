LinkedIn (595/3000)
Do not type the details of your tip into Directory search.

Hush Line’s public Directory is for finding a recipient. Choose a category such as Attorneys or Journalists, or use Search verified users to look for a person you already have in mind. Then open View Profile and read the recipient’s details.

Keep names, events, and document details from your tip out of the search box. Share them only after you have chosen the right recipient and opened that person’s Hush Line message form. You do not need an account to send a message.

Search for the recipient. Save the tip for the message form.

Mastodon (440/500)
The Directory search box is not the place to describe your tip.

Choose Attorneys, Journalists, or another category, or use Search verified users to find a person. Open View Profile before deciding.

Keep names, events, and document details out of search. Once you choose a recipient, put sensitive information in that recipient’s Hush Line message form. No account is required.

Search for the recipient. Save the tip for the message form.
