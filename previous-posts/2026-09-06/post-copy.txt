1. Name the person who checks the Hush Line inbox.
2. Name an absence backup.
3. Choose email notifications for the appropriate role.
4. Choose whether email includes encrypted tip contents.
5. Confirm “PGP key configured” beside the delivery address.

Several addresses can receive notices. Document the task attached to those addresses.

Add the responsibility note to your intake instructions today.
