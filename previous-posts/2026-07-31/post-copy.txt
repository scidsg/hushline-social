When an email is part of a sensitive intake review, recipients and staff need a clear check before they reply, forward, or hand off the message.

Hush Line's Email Validation tool lets staff paste Raw Email Headers, run Validate Headers, and Download Report. The result can show whether the email appears to originate from the stated sender.

That supports a plain ISO 37002 principle: reports should be handled properly and in a timely way. A small repeatable check helps teams slow down at the right moment without turning every review into guesswork.

Build the header check into the staff workflow before an email changes the next step.
